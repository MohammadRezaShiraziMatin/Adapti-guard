E    Labeling by Differential Replay
Our first labeling attempt marked an output as injected if
it contained the injected string verbatim. Because Agent-
Dojo serializes tool outputs as YAML, multi-line injections
are re-wrapped and escaped, and every output carrying the
important_instructions attack was labeled benign. Dif-
ferential replay (same ground-truth call, clean versus injected
environment) avoids the problem; with it, each of the three
AD-Matched templates contributes the same number of in-
jected outputs. We note the pitfall because substring labeling
is the obvious approach and silently inflates false-positive
rates.
