## Why this structure should help citation (and what no revision can promise)
- A named, copyable artifact (a checklist with pass criteria) gives other authors something concrete to cite when they report which items they satisfy.
- Headline numbers in the abstract and one table make the findings easy to quote.
- Citing the nearest 2026 work (and being consistent with it) places the paper inside an active conversation; the claims are framed as complementary, not competing.
- Open artifacts with a one-command reproduction support reuse.
- Citations also depend on the venue, timing, the strength of the confirmatory results and visibility (a preprint with a clear title); none of these can be guaranteed by editing.
